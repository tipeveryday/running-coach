Load status in use: ACWR, 8 weeks

Bars take their status color from the band they land in; the band edges are labeled.

Decisions. (1) Color means intensity, icons mean workout type, length means distance. The calendar, HR zones and pace zones all share the Z1–Z5 scale, so "orange" means threshold everywhere. (2) HR zones keep the cool-to-hot order Garmin users expect, but the steps are re-picked so green and amber stay apart under color blindness. (3) Categorical slot 1 is ember, meaning "you / actual". Comparison series take slots 2–6 in a fixed order and never cycle. (4) No dual-axis charts. The respiration + SpO₂ chart becomes two small charts.

04 · Typography

Inter, 12 steps instead of 28

One family with optical sizing: tight and crisp at 26px, open and readable at 13px. Weights are 400, 500 and 600, with 700 reserved for big numbers. Nothing smaller than 11px, and 11px only for uppercase labels and axis ticks.

Numbers: always tabular

Paces, times, splits and anything in a column use tnum, so digits line up and don't jitter when values update.

Tabular ✓
  1. Lap 14:11
  2. Lap 24:07
  3. Lap 33:58
  4. Lap 44:01
Proportional ✕
  1. Lap 14:11
  2. Lap 24:07
  3. Lap 33:58
  4. Lap 44:01

Rules

  • Units are smaller and quieter than the value: 4:03 /km.
  • Headings are sentence case. Uppercase is reserved for 11px overlines.
  • Coach prose uses body (15/23) with a 68-character max line length. The single most important line is set in lead (17/26).
  • Chart labels are 12px caption in --text-3; axis ticks are 11px. They never take the series color.
  • Never bold whole paragraphs. Bold is for the one phrase that matters.

05 · Spacing, layout, grid

A 4px base, 11 steps

Down from 35 spacing values today. Cards use 20–24px padding, card grids use 16–24px gaps, and sections are 48–64px apart.

Breakpoints & containers

1024–1279Laptop · 12-col grid · top nav · 24px gutter
≥ 1280Desktop · 1240px max container · 32px gutter

Card patterns. There are three: the stat card (number + delta + sparkline + one-line read), the chart card (title, a one-line takeaway, then the chart, then the legend), and the narrative card (coach callouts, with prose capped at 68 characters a line). Everything else is a combination of these. Nested cards are not allowed; inside a card, use an inset well on --surface-2.

Why. In dark mode, shadows barely show, so lift comes from a lighter surface plus a hairline edge. Light mode adds soft shadow. Borders: 1px --border for decoration, --border-input (≥ 3:1) for anything you type into, and a 2px --focus-ring on everything focusable.

07 · Motion

Fast, functional, and off when asked

TokenDurationUse
--dur-1100msHover, press, color change
--dur-2160msTooltip, toggle, tab indicator
--dur-3240msSheet, drawer, expand/collapse
--dur-4360msFirst chart reveal only

Easing: standard for state changes, enter (decelerate) for things arriving, exit (accelerate) for things leaving.

Try it

  • Animate state, not data refreshes. A Garmin sync updates values in place, without re-drawing every chart.
  • No looping animation except progress indicators.
  • Reduced motion: no movement, fades under 100ms.

08 · Iconography

Lucide, one stroke weight, no emoji

Lucide (ISC license) matches today's line-icon style and covers fitness terms well. I'll copy only the ~40 icons we use into a local sprite, so there's no runtime dependency.

Rules. 16, 20 or 24px only; stroke 1.75; currentColor only. An icon that conveys meaning gets an aria-label; a decorative icon gets aria-hidden. The emoji used today (💬 💡 👋 ❤️) are replaced. An icon never appears without a label except in the top bar, where it gets a tooltip and an accessible name.

Placement rules

  • On the chart: labels of 4 words or fewer pinned to one point or range ("Sick", "Race", "Threshold block"). At most 3 per chart, placed in reserved space above the plot so they never cover data.
  • Beside the chart: anything that needs a sentence. It's linked to the chart by a numbered marker, and wraps below the chart on phones.
  • Hover or tap is only for per-point detail. A coaching conclusion never lives in a tooltip.
  • Every chart gets a one-line takeaway under its title and a text alternative.

Copy rules

✓ Do
Hold volume flat this week. ACWR is 1.38, just above the 1.3 sweet spot.
✕ Don't
⚠️ WARNING!!! Your acute:chronic workload ratio is elevated which may increase injury risk.
  • Lead with the action, then the reason. Use "you"; say "I" only in chat.
  • Numbers always have units. Say how confident you are in words ("likely", "about") and as a range.
  • No exclamation marks or emoji. The severity label carries the urgency.
