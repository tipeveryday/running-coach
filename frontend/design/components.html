Domain

InsightCallout · CoachTip

Variants: lead (the page's one decision), card, inline (inside a card) and pinned (numbered, linked to a chart marker). Each severity pairs an icon with a word.

Domain

MetricTrend

Sparkline, value, delta and a plain-words interpretation. The delta is colored by whether the change is good for that metric, and always carries an arrow and a sign.

Domain

AnnotatedChart

One wrapper for every chart. Labels sit in a lane above the plot, target labels sit in the right margin, and notes sit beside the chart. Focus a chart and use ← → to read values; every chart can be opened as a table.

Domain

ForecastChart

Actual line solid, projection dashed, with nested 50% and 80% ranges, a "Today" split, the goal labeled outside the data, and an always-visible "What this means".

Domain

CalendarGrid · WorkoutCell

Color shows intensity, the icon shows workout type, the bar shows distance. Done, missed, unplanned, rest, race and today each have a non-color cue. Arrow keys move between days; on narrow screens the month becomes compact instead of scrolling sideways.

Domain

ActivityDeepDive

Reading order: what the session was, what it means, the evidence, then the coaching notes. Opens in a sheet from the calendar or log; shown inline here.
